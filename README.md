## What does ChatGPT Pro include?

OpenAI describes ChatGPT Pro as a paid plan for users who rely on advanced AI capabilities for complex work. The Pro feature set includes access to Pro models, Codex, deep research, image creation, memory, and file uploads.

The practical value depends on how you work. For a casual user, the feature list can look impressive while the actual monthly usage remains modest. For someone who uses ChatGPT as a coding assistant, research tool, document analyst, or long-running work companion, usage limits can become the deciding factor.

Current Pro-related capabilities include:

- Pro model access for difficult reasoning tasks.
- Codex for coding workflows.
- Deep research features.
- Image creation.
- Memory and file uploads.
- Access to GPT-6 Pro on eligible Pro tiers.
- Higher usage allowances than lower-priced plans.
- Additional access to ChatGPT Work and Codex features, with separate usage rules.

The presence of a feature in the plan does not mean it is unlimited. OpenAI specifically states that Pro subscriptions have usage limits for some models. When a model allowance is reached, the model may be temporarily unavailable until the allowance resets. ChatGPT displays the reset time when available.

That point matters because “Pro” is not a magic word that removes every limit. It gives you a larger allowance and access to higher-level capabilities, but usage is still governed by the plan, model, product surface, and account rules.

## Pro 100: the lower-cost way to access Pro features

At **$100 per month**, Pro 100 is the entry point for the current ChatGPT Pro lineup. It includes the main Pro feature family, but with a lower usage allowance than Pro 200 and Pro 500. Ultrafast access is not included.

OpenAI’s current documentation says Pro 100 provides higher usage than Plus, while Pro 200 provides more usage than Pro 100. In a separate explanation of Pro usage, OpenAI describes the older Pro structure as Pro 100 offering approximately five times the Plus usage level, though exact allowances can vary by model and product.

Pro 100 makes the most sense when:

- You need access to Pro-level models for specific projects.
- You use advanced reasoning several times per week rather than all day.
- You need Codex or deep research but do not constantly run long tasks.
- The $200 monthly price feels difficult to justify.
- You are comfortable waiting for a reset after reaching a model allowance.

It is less attractive if you regularly run multiple demanding workflows at once. A plan that looks inexpensive compared with Pro 200 can become frustrating when your main model is temporarily unavailable during a deadline.

The sensible approach is to estimate your actual usage before upgrading. Consider how often you use advanced models, how many long documents you process, how frequently you run coding tasks, and whether you regularly upload large files. If most of your sessions are short questions, Pro 100 may already be more than you need.

## Pro 200: more headroom for demanding individual use

**Pro 200 costs $200 per month** and includes more usage than Pro 100. It does not include Astra Ultrafast, which remains exclusive to Pro 500.

OpenAI’s current documentation also contains an important detail about Pro 200. New Pro 200 subscriptions are available again, but new subscribers who are not eligible for grandfathering receive a lower included usage allowance than some earlier Pro 200 users. The price remains $200 per month.

That means old comparisons based on a previous “20x” allowance may not describe the experience of every new subscriber. The plan name and price stayed the same, but the included allowance can depend on eligibility and subscription history.

Pro 200 is usually the more practical tier for users who:

- Use ChatGPT for several hours on working days.
- Work with code, research, analysis, or large files regularly.
- Need more room for Pro models than Pro 100 provides.
- Want a high-capacity personal plan without paying $500 per month.
- Prefer to keep the same subscription for demanding projects rather than switching plans frequently.

There is another distinction worth keeping in mind. A Pro 200 subscription does not automatically provide Pro 500 features. Keeping a previous Pro 200 allowance does not add Ultrafast access, and Pro 200 remains a separate plan.

If you already have Pro 200, be careful before canceling. OpenAI says eligible existing subscribers may retain their previous allowance through **October 29, 2026**, while the subscription remains active. After that date, affected subscriptions move to the lower included allowance while the monthly price remains $200.

## Pro 500: the premium tier with Astra Ultrafast

The new **Pro 500 plan costs $500 per month**. It has the highest included usage among the three Pro plans and is currently the only tier with Astra Ultrafast.

Ultrafast is designed for users who value faster service in supported workflows. OpenAI says it is available in ChatGPT Work and Codex, and that usage first draws from the plan’s included allowance before using any available credit balance. Buying credits on Pro 100 or Pro 200 does not unlock Ultrafast.

At $500 per month, Pro 500 is difficult to justify for ordinary chat use. It is aimed at a narrower group:

- Developers running heavy Codex workflows.
- Professionals handling long-running projects every day.
- Teams or individuals who need the largest personal Pro allowance.
- Users for whom faster service has a direct financial value.
- People who would otherwise lose productive time waiting for limits or slower processing.

The math is straightforward. Pro 500 costs five times as much as Pro 100 and 2.5 times as much as Pro 200. The plan only makes financial sense when the extra allowance or Ultrafast access prevents enough lost time to cover that difference.

For most individual users, Pro 500 should be considered a specialist plan rather than the default upgrade.

## Current model access and usage limits

ChatGPT Pro pricing is tied to more than storage or a larger feature checklist. Model access and usage limits are part of the value proposition.

OpenAI currently lists GPT-6 Pro as available on Pro 100 and Pro 200, as well as Business and Enterprise plans. The company also states that GPT-6 Pro has usage limits and that a Pro subscription does not provide unlimited access to that model.

For the current GPT-6 Pro allowance described by OpenAI:

- Pro 200 includes 200 messages per week.
- GPT-5.6 Sol Pro usage is handled separately or through shared rules depending on the tier.
- Reaching a model limit does not end the subscription.
- Users must wait for the allowance to reset or use another available model.

OpenAI notes that usage allowances can differ between ChatGPT, Work, and Codex. This is an easy detail to miss when comparing plans. A subscription may provide one allowance for Chat, another for Codex, and separate rules for ChatGPT Work.

The result is that monthly price alone does not tell you how much usable capacity you receive. Before subscribing, check the allowance for the specific model and product surface you plan to use.

For many users, the answer is no. ChatGPT Pro is expensive compared with Plus, and the higher tiers only become rational when you use advanced features frequently enough to encounter restrictions.

### Pro 100 is worth considering if:

- You want Pro models for serious but intermittent work.
- You use deep research or Codex regularly but not continuously.
- You need more capacity than Plus without committing to $200 or $500.
- Your work can tolerate waiting for usage resets.

### Pro 200 is a better fit if:

- ChatGPT is part of your daily professional workflow.
- You frequently write, code, research, analyze files, or manage long tasks.
- You want a larger allowance and fewer interruptions.
- The cost can be connected to billable work or measurable productivity.

### Pro 500 may be justified if:

- You use ChatGPT and Codex intensively throughout the day.
- Faster service affects deadlines or output.
- You have tested lower tiers and repeatedly hit their limits.
- The additional cost is smaller than the value of your saved time.

A useful rule is to start from your workflow, not from the feature list. If you cannot name the specific limitation that Pro will solve, upgrading may simply buy you a more expensive subscription that you use in the same way.

## Important account and billing details

OpenAI does not allow every type of subscription behavior. Its help documentation says that sharing account credentials, reselling access, and automatically extracting data can violate the Terms of Use and may result in restrictions.

That makes the purchase method worth checking carefully. A service that subscribes to your own account is materially different from a service that gives multiple people access to a shared account. Before entering account information, understand which arrangement is being offered and whether it fits OpenAI’s rules.

Also avoid subscribing through multiple platforms at the same time. OpenAI warns that purchasing on the web, Apple App Store, and Google Play can create duplicate active subscriptions and duplicate charges.

For cancellation, uninstalling the app is not enough. You must cancel through the platform where the subscription was purchased. A canceled plan normally remains active until the end of its current billing period.
